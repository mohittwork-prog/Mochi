Mochi types when you do.

A browser prototype of a floating desktop cat. Type anywhere on this page and its paws press the same keys on its keyboard. Move your mouse, stroke its head, or leave it alone for a while.

The cat has no background of its own, so as a desktop widget it will float over whatever is on screen. Nothing you type leaves this page; keys are only used to animate the paws. A Mac version would need a native app to see keys typed in other programs.
